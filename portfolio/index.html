OpenFAIR-Mat v0.1.0 (MIT License)

Lightweight, zero-external-API pre-flight validator for federated materials and scientific datasets. Detects broken crystal geometries, atom-count mismatches, non-finite values, impossible physics, and mixed units before repository upload.

  • Repository: github.com/ttthkim-hue/openfair-mat
  • Validators: CIF (metric tensor & occupancy), VASP POSCAR (3×3 det volume & counts), CSV/JSON (NaN/Inf, eV/meV, K/C, Å/nm).
  • Packaging: Auto-generates RO-Crate 1.1 (ro-crate-metadata.json) & SHA-256 repro-manifest.json.
  • Schema Targets: Zenodo (CERN), NOMAD (FAIRmat), Materials Project readiness checks.
  • Observed Verification: 19/19 pytest cases passing in 2.10s; 90.65% coverage; CLI exit 0 (valid) vs 1 (invalid).
